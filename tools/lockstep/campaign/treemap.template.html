Tiles are sized by countable blocks: the blocks that can run, leaving out those that only fail an assertion or that the machine code shows no input reaches. A block is verified once lockstep has run it in the original and the port and their results agreed. Under 3a a function counts as verified at ≥90% of its blocks; under 3b only at all of them, or with the rest explained in the gap ledger. “To review” means mismatches only under mutated inputs, not yet explained. Click an area or unit to zoom in; click the path above to zoom out. The round slider shows the map after each round; earlier rounds are counted again from their own results, with ports changed since then counting as they stood at that round.
